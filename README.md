The RPV Water Delivery System is designed to make water delivery operations easier, faster, and more organized by providing a centralized dashboard for managing daily tasks. Through the system, the admin can assign deliveries by entering customer details, location, and order information while also selecting and managing drivers in an efficient way. It also helps in tracking business performance by showing income summaries for daily, weekly, and monthly earnings, along with net profit, total income, and expenses such as fuel. The system keeps a record of all delivery history so transactions can be easily reviewed and searched when needed. In addition, it provides a visual analytics chart that helps monitor profit trends over time. Overall, the system improves the workflow of water delivery operations by reducing manual work, improving accuracy, and making it easier to manage deliveries, drivers, and business performance in one place.
